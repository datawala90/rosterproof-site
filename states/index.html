State Medicaid exclusion lists, all in one place

42 states + DC · 88,826 active records · updated weekly · as of 2026-10-30

Every state Medicaid program can exclude or terminate providers on its own authority, and most publish a list. The lists come in 7 different formats on 43 different schedules, and about 54.1% of their active records never appear on the federal OIG LEIE, so a federal-only screen misses them. RosterProof loads every list below weekly into one schema, so one check covers all of them. Each state page shows the official source, the update schedule, the counts, and how to screen against it.
