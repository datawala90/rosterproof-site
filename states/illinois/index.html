What the federal gap means in Illinois

Of the 3,245 active records on the Illinois list, 67.4% do not appear on the federal OIG LEIE. A program that screens only the federal lists, as 42 CFR 455.436 requires at minimum, never sees those providers. Under ACA §6501, an exclusion or for-cause termination in one state's Medicaid program is grounds for termination in every other state, so a Illinois exclusion matters to any payer or employer in any state. The earliest exclusion start date the agency publishes is 1979-04-19 and the most recent is 2026-10-07. 329 of the records belong to providers that at least one other jurisdiction has also excluded. How to check a provider against the Illinois list

  1. In the browser, no account: paste or upload NPIs on Screen my providers. Each NPI is checked against the Illinois list and every other list at once; results download as CSV.
  2. By API: POST /v1/screen with NPIs, names or organizations; a free key allows 500 lookups a month. API documentation.
  3. In your warehouse: the free Snowflake listing or the weekly CSV / Parquet download; filter JURISDICTION = 'IL' in EXCLUSIONS_SCREENABLE.
  4. Then confirm with the agency. A hit identifies a published record and, where present, an NPI match with a stated confidence. Before any adverse action, verify against the official Illinois list (and OIG's SSN-based search for LEIE matches).
