What the data says

medicaid exclusion statistics · figures as of July 2026, reproducible from the dataset

Every number on this page was computed from the RosterProof unified exclusion dataset — the federal OIG LEIE plus 36 state and district Medicaid exclusion lists, resolved to NPIs against the CMS NPPES registry. These are analytical findings from the July 2026 edition; each is reproducible from the published dataset, and each carries its own honest caveat.

The ten-month median between first and second state is the window where cross-state screening catches what single-state screening cannot — under ACA §6501, one state's exclusion is every state's problem.

Caveat: NPI-verified multi-jurisdiction matches; jurisdictions counted distinctly (federal counts once).

1 in 340

actively-registered US individual providers is under an active healthcare exclusion that we have verified against the NPI registry — 21,639 of 7.37 million active individual NPIs.

Caveat: this is a floor. It counts only exclusions we can tie to an active NPI with registry-verified identity; most state exclusion records publish no NPI at all, so the true rate is higher.
